Before you write

Good to know

How long does a piece take?

It depends entirely on length and complexity. A short sequence can be weeks; the channel's most ambitious film took a year. I'll give you a real estimate once I've seen the brief.

Do you take fan-work commissions?

Yes — it's most of what I do. I'll be upfront about anything that crosses into rights territory you'd need to clear yourself.

Will you join a collab?

Often. Guest sequences in multi-animator projects are some of my favourite work. The Discord is usually the fastest way to start that conversation.

What do you need from me?

The fight, a rough runtime, your deadline, and any references or music you're set on. Everything else we can work out together.

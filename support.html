Why it matters

Hand-drawn
costs time

There are no rigs and no shortcuts here. A single second of animation is a stack of individual drawings, and the channel's most-watched film took a full year to finish.

Patron support buys the two things that actually change the output: time that would otherwise go elsewhere, and the software, hardware and sound licensing that keep the films looking and sounding the way they should.

It also keeps the work free to watch for everyone else — which is the whole point.

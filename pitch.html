One line. One fight. Sign in, put it on the board, and let everyone else argue about it. The ideas that rise to the top are the ones I animate next.

How it works

Keep it
to a line

  • A hundred characters. Enough for a fight, not enough for a screenplay.
  • Sign in with Google. It is the only way one vote stays one vote.
  • Every pitch is read before it appears. Nothing lands on the board automatically.
  • Replies go up straight away. Sign-in required, and anyone can delete their own.
  • Pitching is not commissioning. A popular idea gets animated when it gets animated.
